## 1. Verdict

**One family ("Product management"), with Product Owner inside it** — same pattern as Scrum Master inside IT Project Manager.
The product needs a **hard edge against the non-digital "product manager"** (banking/insurance/FMCG product), which in HK
is common, not rare.

Why one family (inference from the evidence below):
- Every authority describes the PO duties as a *subset* of PM work, not different work (Scrum.org, Pragmatic, Cagan, SAFe).
- Scrum.org: PO is an accountability, PM is a job title; the same person usually holds both.
- No official taxonomy (O*NET, ANZSCO/OSCA, SG/HK frameworks found) has a separate Product Owner occupation.
- The core-work questions overlap almost fully: "do you own a backlog / decide priorities / define what gets built and why?"
  A PO answers yes to these; a strategy-heavy PM answers yes plus market/discovery questions.

Split it later only if tailoring quality shows PO CVs being pushed toward market-strategy language they cannot back.

## 2. Definitions

**Product Owner (Scrum Guide 2020)** — source says: "accountable for maximizing the value of the product resulting from the
work of the Scrum Team"; accountable for Product Backlog management: developing and communicating the Product Goal, creating
and communicating backlog items, ordering them, keeping the backlog transparent. "One person, not a committee." It is one of
three "accountabilities", not a job title. https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html

**Product Manager** — source says:
- SAFe: "defining desirable, viable, feasible, and sustainable solutions that meet customer needs"; "aligning product strategy,
  vision, and roadmaps with customer needs"; manages and prioritises the ART (team-of-teams) backlog.
  https://framework.scaledagile.com/product-management
- Cagan/SVPG: the PM is responsible for value and viability and "leading a product"; the PO part is "just a very minor part"
  of the PM job; CSPO training "prepared them only for the Agile rituals the product owner has to perform".
  https://svpg.com/product-manager-vs-product-owner-revisited/
- Pragmatic Institute: PO responsibilities are "just a small subset of product management"; PO elaborates user stories,
  manages sprint-level backlog, interprets product vision; bridges the PM's market focus and the team's need for detail.
  https://www.pragmaticinstitute.com/resources/podcasts/product/product-owner-vs-product-manager/

What each produces (inference): PO → ordered backlog, user stories/acceptance criteria, sprint goals, accepted increments.
PM → product vision, roadmap, prioritisation rationale, discovery/market insight, business case, outcome metrics, plus the
backlog when no separate PO exists.

## 3. Overlap and difference

- Overlap: owning what gets built and why, prioritisation, backlog, stakeholder trade-offs, accepting work.
- Difference (source says): SAFe deliberately splits the Scrum PO job — PM owns market/strategy and the ART backlog, PO sits
  inside one agile team and owns the team backlog/stories. Together they do what one Scrum PO does.
  https://v5.scaledagileframework.com/?p=52697 (SAFe representative's comment thread)
- Scrum.org: "a Product Manager is a job title, while a Product Owner is an accountability"; the PO accountability "may be
  fulfilled by someone who is a Product Manager". https://www.scrum.org/node/77033 ,
  https://www.scrum.org/node/98762 (search snippets; the exact node-to-quote mapping not individually verified)
- Disagreement among authorities: Cagan says PO is a delivery role; Scrum purists say the PO is meant to be the full
  value-owner and the delivery-admin reading is a misuse. Both camps agree it's the same *kind* of work, they disagree on
  how much of it a "PO" usually gets. https://pablopernot.fr/en/2023/04/po-pm-la-grande-embrouille

Neighbour boundary (inference): a backlog-only "PO" who mostly writes stories is close to Business Analyst; the PO who
facilitates ceremonies and tracks delivery is close to Scrum Master (IT PM family). That is why the family's yes-questions
must centre on *deciding* priority/scope, not *writing* specs or *running* delivery.

  (2025-2026), describing strategy + roadmap for an agile team with a Scrum Master. A Hang Seng "Digital Product Manager"
  ad asks the holder to "act as Digital Product Owner (PO)" — the two titles used for one job in one ad.
  https://apply.careers.hsbc.com/job/Mongkok-Senior-Product-Owner-Hang-Seng-Bank-%28HK%29-Kowl/1329430257
  https://apply.careers.hsbc.com/job/Central-Product-Owner-Hong/1348206257
  https://mycareer.hsbc.com/fr_FR/external/PipelineDetail/Digital-Platform-Manager-Digital-Onboarding-Hang-Seng-Bank-HK/248571
  Standard Chartered uses "Hive Lead" for a PO-type role: https://mygwork.com/jobs/standard-chartered-bank-director-hive-lead-wealth-liquidity-leverage-hive-hong-kong-singapore-6274907
- Morgan McKinley HK lists "Product Owner" under Technology (median 55,000/month, currency presumably HKD).
  https://www.morganmckinley.com/hk/salary-guide/data/product-owner/hong-kong-sar

  but no source quantified PO vs PM share by region — I found no survey breaking it out.

## 5. Official taxonomies

- O*NET: "Product Manager" and "Product Marketing Manager" are reported titles under **11-2021 Marketing Managers**
  (alongside Brand Manager); "Product Owner" not listed. https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-2021.00
  Inference: O*NET lumps product with marketing, i.e. it does not model digital product management at all — useless as a
  family boundary.
- ANZSCO / OSCA (Australia): no product manager or product owner occupation found; nearest are ICT Project Manager 135112
  (delivery) and Other ICT Managers 135199. ANZSCO is superseded by OSCA 2024.
  https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/occupation-and-industry-profiles/occupations-anzsco/135112-ict-project-managers
  https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/classifications/osca-occupation-standard-classification-australia/latest-release
  (not verified by browsing OSCA's search for "product").
- Singapore Skills Framework for ICT (IMDA/SSG): lists "Product Management" as a navigation item; exact track and job-role
  names not confirmed. https://www.imda.gov.sg/-/media/imtalent-portal-revamp/3-guidances/skills-planning/sf-ict/ict-navigation-tool-2020.pdf
- HK Qualifications Framework: no ICT product manager standard found; the banking SCS has a "Product Development and Brand
  Marketing" functional area and "Product Development Officer" roles — i.e. the *financial-product* meaning.
  https://hkqf.gov.hk/files/page/banking-vqp/1/Final_Report_on_Banking_VQP_03.07.16_(revised)-1742284998.pdf
- Summary: no taxonomy found treats PO and PM as two occupations; none treats PO as an occupation at all.

## 6. Scope edge and word pollution

Proposed scope sentence (inference): *Owns what a digital/technology product or service does and why — product vision,
roadmap, priorities and the backlog a delivery team builds from — and answers for its outcomes. Includes Product Owner,
Technical Product Manager, Digital Product Manager, Platform PM, Group PM / Head of Product. Excludes financial, insurance
and consumer-goods "product managers" who design the commercial offer (rates, fees, coverage, pricing, brand) rather than
software; product marketing managers (go-to-market, positioning); and delivery roles (scrum master, delivery lead → IT
Project Manager) and requirements roles (BA).*

Word pollution — source says, HK: "Product Manager" ads for non-software financial products are plentiful:
- OCBC "Product Manager, Consumer Secured Lending" (mortgages, insurance financing). https://jobs.ctgoodjobs.hk/job/09457976/product-manager-consumer-secured-lending
- BOCHK "Senior Wealth Management Product Manager (Insurance)". https://pt.ctgoodjobs.hk/job/60406673/senior-wealth-management-product-manager-insurance
- HSBC "Product Manager - HK Business" (premium financing). https://apply.careers.hsbc.com/job/Central-Product-Manager-HK-Business-Hong/1362888557/
- DBS bancassurance product management. https://careers.cfainstitute.org/job/9128249/specialist-product-management-bancassurance-consumer-banking/
- Singapore: bank deposit/card/loan PM ads and a Citi "Investment Product Manager". https://singapore.careers.cfainstitute.org/job/9163047/investment-product-manager/
- O*NET itself files PM next to Brand Manager under marketing.
FMCG: the HK/SG title there is usually "Brand Manager", not "Product Manager" (one Randstad HK FMCG ad seen was Brand
Manager) — lower risk than banking/insurance. https://www.randstad.com.hk/jobs/c1-product-marketing

Inference: in HK and SG, where banking/insurance employers dominate, a meaningful share of "Product Manager" ads are
financial-product roles. Title alone cannot route them; the family classifier must read the body (software/platform/app/
backlog/engineers/sprints vs rates/coverage/sales/regulatory product approval). Edge cases: "Card Product Manager" at a
fintech (Crypto.com) and "Digital Product Manager" in a bank are mixed — route on whether they own a backlog for a tech team.
