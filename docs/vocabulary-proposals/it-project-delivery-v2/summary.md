## What the widening does, and the caveat to weigh

v1's scope already swallows the agile delivery titles (scrum master, delivery lead, release
manager). v2 adds one sentence: product ownership — owning a backlog, setting sprint/release
priorities, steering what the delivery team builds next — is inside the family. **First, the
labeler-fault check the runbook requires: this is NOT a labeler fault.** Against v1's scope
(deliver the technology work), "no family fits" for Product Owner was a defensible reading —
PO decides *what* gets built, delivery decides *how and when*. That is exactly why this is an
owner decision.

**The caveat, stated plainly:** the floor questions stay delivery-shaped (own delivery end to end,
control risks). A product owner can answer them, but from the edge of their role, not its centre.
If POs deserve their own questions, the honest home is a future product-management family instead —
and this widening should be **rejected**, with the labels counted as answered. Both are defensible;
the package implements the widening in case you want it.
