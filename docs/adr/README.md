# Architecture decision records — index

One row per ADR, to pick which to read. A new or amended ADR updates its row here in the same commit.

| ADR | Title | Status | Decision | Amends / amended by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [0001](0001-growth-rule-for-structured-facts.md) | The growth rule: adding a new kind of structured fact | Accepted | Each new kind of fact gets its own designed shape, is stored beside existing data with no migration, and never loses its source sentence. | Verification superseded by 0006 clause 10; rule 4 scope-noted by 0007 |
| [0002](0002-how-a-structured-fact-reaches-the-cv.md) | How a structured fact reaches the printed CV | Accepted | Provenance decides the bridge: stored parts may only be reformatted or computed on, the person's words may be written up, and invented precision is forbidden. | Clause 2 amended by 0016 |
| [0003](0003-the-shared-parts-organisation-date-level.md) | The shared parts: an organisation, a date, a level | Accepted | One organisation record serves as employer, school and issuer (the role lives on the link), with shared date and level parts and corrections that supersede, never replace. | — |
| [0004](0004-each-elements-own-parts.md) | Each element's own parts: work history, education, skills, certifications, languages | Accepted | Defines each element's fields; a skill keeps the term as written plus a normalised term, and every fact points at its origin (clause 1a). | — |
| [0005](0005-a-stretch-belongs-to-its-advert.md) | A stretch belongs to its advert | Accepted | An approved stretch attaches to the one application it was made for, never to the person's profile, so it cannot leak into later CVs. | — |
| [0006](0006-a-project-is-a-container-not-a-fact.md) | A project is a container, not a fact | Accepted | A personal project is a container in a section named `Projects`, holding its own facts. | Supersedes 0001's Verification; clause 5 extended by 0009 |
| [0007](0007-what-prints-is-decided-per-application.md) | What prints is decided per application, not per fact | Accepted | No fact is unprintable: everything is held, and a per-market withholding pass decides what prints for each advert. | Scope note on 0001 rule 4 ("not an amendment") |
| [0008](0008-how-a-fact-arrives-read-worked-out-or-asked.md) | How a fact arrives: read, worked out, or asked | Accepted | Every fact arrives read, worked out or asked, fixed per fact (not per visitor), and the machine never asks for a value it will regenerate. | — |
| [0009](0009-a-work-project-stays-a-bullet.md) | A project done for an employer stays a bullet | Accepted | A work project or client gets no named container inside a job; it stays inside the bullet text. | Extends 0006 clause 5 |
| [0010](0010-what-we-cannot-classify-is-kept-printed-and-reviewed-by-kind.md) | What we cannot classify is kept, printed faithfully, and reviewed by kind | Accepted | A CV block with no decided home is kept whole with its origin, printed faithfully, and given a machine-proposed kind the owner curates. | — |
| [0011](0011-a-hole-is-asked-blank-or-advert-triggered.md) | A hole is asked now, shown as a blank, or asked when an advert makes it matter | Accepted | A missing fact is asked now only if it changes what the machine can do, otherwise shown as a never-printed blank, or asked when an advert tests it. | Clause 2 amended by 0016 |
| [0012](0012-a-merged-bullet-declares-the-result-it-kept.md) | A merged bullet declares the result it kept | Accepted | A bullet merging several claims carries an `outcome` that must appear in the printed text; with no stated result, it does not merge. | — |
| [0013](0013-the-master-cv-improves-on-the-document-it-came-from.md) | The master CV improves on the document it came from | Accepted | The master CV may move a fact to the section a reader expects, but adds only what is already in the person's own material. | Clause 2 amended by 0016 |
| [0014](0014-the-job-labeler.md) | A past job is labeled with a job family by an LLM against a closed list, stored on the record, and trusted only after measurement | accepted | An LLM places each job in a family from a closed versioned list (never the nearest guess), stored on the record and trusted only once measured. | Self-amended 3 times: multi-family and never asks (1), relatedness as a group tree (2), employer looked up once (3) |
| [0015](0015-a-family-floor-may-name-equipment-a-family-scope-may-not.md) | A family floor may name equipment; a family scope may not | accepted | A family-floor question may name a concrete tool when demand earns it; the family's scope sentence never may. | Amended by 0016 (the floor feeds the drafter; the review gates the reveal) |
| [0016](0016-the-cv-is-reviewed-not-asked.md) | The CV is reviewed, not asked | Accepted | Discovery asks only eligibility questions; a mandatory review of the CV, with unticked drafted suggestions, replaces the family-floor questions. | Amends 0002 cl. 2, 0011 cl. 2, 0013 cl. 2, 0015 (floor feeds the drafter); clause 6 amended by 0017 |
| [0017](0017-the-cv-chat-proposes-from-the-persons-own-words.md) | The CV chat proposes, from the person's own words only | Accepted | A chat opened from a door on one thing or one section proposes changes to the master CV from the person's words only — no stretch, nothing applied without a tap, the transcript not kept. | Amends 0016 cl. 6; replaces the Add-something-new flow |
