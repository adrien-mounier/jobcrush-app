The card already answers most of it

Same ring, same gold ticks, same grey ? for the gap, same language. The checklist did not invent a visual world — it inherited this one. Nothing on the left needs restyling.

What the card does not show: why

The card names the fact (“7+ years delivering IT systems”). The checklist names the requirement and the sentence that satisfied it. On a card you are skimming past, the fact is right. On the screen where you commit to writing a CV, the reason is the thing you check.

What the card does not show: weight

The card sorts into fit and not-yet. The checklist sorts into Essential and Desirable, so a missing essential reads differently from a missing nice-to-have. The card carries that only as two numbers in the breakdown grid, below the fold.

The card has one thing the checklist dropped

The match breakdown — four cells and a Strong/Partial/Weak pill — is a shipped component. The checklist's summary line was cut as a repeat of its group counts, which it was; but this grid is the same information with somewhere to live.
