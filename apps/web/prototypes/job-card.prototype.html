Ticket #10 — the job card. Same job, same facts, three shapes: A the ad plus a number (a job board with a score bolted on — the control §3 has to beat), B the verdict (their ask, your fact, the ad demoted), C the scorecard (their requirement list, banded, no prose). Swipe or press. Right takes you into the live card, where answering moves the number — and where a no still pays.
