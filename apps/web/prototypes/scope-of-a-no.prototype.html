Design C — the scope of a “no”

The machine offers a stretch, or asks a question. She says no. Which no? “Not for this job” and “never” are different, and a misread turns “not today” into a permanent silence. Three structures for where the permanent no is allowed to exist — same wording discipline in all three.

Play the loop: decline the stretch, skip the question, and watch what the second job (three weeks later) does with your no. Then open the profile — every permanent no must be listed there, one tap back on. Judge: could you tell what each button would do before tapping it? · did the permanent one feel mistappable? · where would you look to undo it?
