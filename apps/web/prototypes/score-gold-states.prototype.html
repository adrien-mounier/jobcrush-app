The score earns its gold — four treatments of the high end

You picked the score out of 100. All four dials below are that same score — they differ only in how the dial celebrates as the number climbs. Every one uses the same ladder: below 50 the arc is quiet steel · 50–74 bronze · 75–89 the house gold · 90+ is the “top match” state, and that's where the four personalities split.

Drag the slider (or use the presets) and watch all four cross 90 together. The treatments are cumulative ideas — the winner can borrow from its neighbours.

Score 77/100
A · Heat — the metal ladder alone

The quietest option: nothing is added, the arc simply becomes more precious — steel → bronze → gold → at 90+ the arc and the number both go bright gold with a soft glow on the stroke. Reads instantly at a distance.
B · Halo — at 90+ the dial breathes

Same metal ladder, plus life: at 90+ a warm halo breathes behind the dial and a bright spark orbits the arc. The screen's one living object — your eye goes there first. The risk: it keeps moving forever.
C · Badge — the pick — at 90+ a “Top match” badge appears
★ Top match

The chosen design. The metal ladder does the work, and at 90+ the number turns bright gold and a still “★ Top match” badge appears beneath — an earned emblem, no animation. The badge is also the one element that can travel to other screens later (job lists, cards) without the dial.
D · Forged — the number itself turns to gold

Crossing 90 fires a one-time burst of gold sparks, then the number itself becomes forged gold — a slow sheen passes through the digits. The celebration happens once, at the moment of crossing; what remains is a precious but calm object.
