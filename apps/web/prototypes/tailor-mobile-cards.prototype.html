#157 · Design B round 3c — mobile deck

Same content and rules as the desktop merge: the same job, the same eight asks, the same physics (a fact flies to Profile, a stretch stays with the advert). Only the shape changes — one spine, one card at a time, and the CV lives as a sheet you pull up from the bottom.

New in 3c (your note): the progress bar opens. Tap it and the eight things this advert asks for are listed underneath, marked you have this / expired / not yet, each satisfied one opening to show its evidence. It only reports — an unanswered ask isn't even tappable, because the cards are where answering happens. The list keeps its own scroll so it can never squeeze the card.

The creative ask — the ending. Tap through to the last card ("What I changed"), then tap Continue: the card settles down rather than sliding away, a soft pulse travels to the CV bar below and it glows once with a two-and-a-half-second cue ("Pull it up for one last look"), then the two exits arrive.

Try it: drag the bar at the bottom up, or just tap it — the CV rises. Drag or tap again to put it away.
